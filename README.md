# 🌤️ RecoverySky

**RecoverySky** is an **open-source** platform designed to provide a **safe, supportive, and engaging online space** for people in recovery. Built with **community, accountability, and accessibility** in mind, RecoverySky brings the **spirit of in-person meetings** into the digital world, ensuring no one has to recover alone. 💙✨

---

## 🚀 What is RecoverySky?
RecoverySky is a **private social network** for people in **All forms of recovery, including but not limited to 12-step fellowships (NA, AA, and beyond)** to **attend virtual meetings, connect with friends, and build lasting support networks**. Unlike traditional social media, RecoverySky prioritizes **recovery-first interactions** and **anonymity-conscious design**. 

## 🎯 Key Features
- 🏠 **Recovery-Focused Community** – A safe space for people in recovery to **connect, support, and grow** together.
- 🎥 **Virtual Meeting Integration** – Host or attend **secure, anonymous** online meetings.
- 🤝 **Small Group Dynamics (“Packs”)** – Form **tight-knit accountability groups** to attend meetings and check in on each other.
- 🔔 **Daily Check-Ins & Prompts** – Encouraging **consistent engagement** in recovery routines.
- 🔒 **Privacy & Anonymity First** – Designed with **12-step traditions in mind**, ensuring **confidentiality and security**.
- 🆓 **Free to Use** – Core features will **always be free**, with optional **premium features** like **court-verified attendance reports** available.

## 💡 The Vision
RecoverySky is **not** just another social network—it’s a **lifeline**. We aim to **bridge the gap between online and in-person meetings**, offering **tools that empower individuals in recovery** while maintaining the integrity of 12-step traditions. 

We believe that **connection is the key to long-term recovery**, and RecoverySky helps ensure that **no one slips through the cracks**. 🌈✨
